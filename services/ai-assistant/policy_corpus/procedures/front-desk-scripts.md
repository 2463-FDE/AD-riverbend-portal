# Front-desk eligibility scripts (synthetic training)

Replace bracketed labels with cited source facts. Do not invent numbers.

## Active

“Eligibility is active for [product] on [date of service] according to [source title], [section], [version or date]. This is not a guarantee of payment. Network/referral/authorization and cost-share still have to match the same official source.”

## Inactive

“The official source [source title, section, date] shows coverage is not active for this date of service. I cannot treat the card as current. A supervisor can review options for a non-emergency visit.”

## Unknown / insufficient evidence

“I do not have enough current official information to answer. I will not guess. I am sending this to a person to verify with the payer.”

## Conflicting

“Two sources disagree: [source A, section, date] and [source B, section, date]. I will not blend them. A person will verify with the payer before I give a coverage answer.”

## Unavailable / payer downtime

“The official eligibility source is not available right now. That is not a denial. For emergency care we do not wait. For a scheduled non-emergency visit, a supervisor will apply clinic financial policy and retry.”

## Patient-disputed

“You believe this result is wrong. I will record the dispute and send it to a person to re-verify. I will not override the official source by guessing.”
