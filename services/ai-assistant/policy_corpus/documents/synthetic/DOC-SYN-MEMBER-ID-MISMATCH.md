## Mismatch types (descriptive only)

- Name order or spelling does not match the payer response.
- Date of birth disagrees.
- Subscriber versus member identifier used in the wrong field.
- Dependent listed on a card but not on the current response.
- Old identifier after a plan replacement.

## Handling

Do not invent a “probably the same person” match. Recollect identifiers, re-inquire once, and if still mismatched, mark unknown/conflict and escalate to a human/payer verification. Do not browse other patients to find a matching identifier.

## Forbidden

No example names, dates of birth, member IDs, or screenshots appear in this training set. Staff must not paste identifiers into the eligibility assistant conversation as if the assistant were a search engine for other patients.
