## Date of service

Eligibility is always for a date of service, not “in general.” A plan that starts tomorrow is not active for today’s visit. A plan that ended yesterday is inactive for today even if the card is still in the wallet.

## Future-dated coverage

If the official source shows a future effective date, say inactive-for-today with that effective date cited. Offer to schedule on/after the effective date only when clinically appropriate and never as a delay of emergency screening.

## Terminated coverage

If the official source shows a termination date on or before the visit date, the result is inactive. Do not soften it to unknown. Offer self-pay/reschedule options per clinic financial policy after non-emergency screening needs are clear.
