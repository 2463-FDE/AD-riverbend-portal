## Required citation elements

Every non-refused eligibility answer must name source title, document ID, section label, and version or effective or retrieval date.

## Stale sources

If the only document is older than the current official version, or is a training summary that conflicts with a current official source, refuse a definitive answer and escalate.

## Conflicts

Cite both, state the conflict, do not blend, route to a human/payer verification.
