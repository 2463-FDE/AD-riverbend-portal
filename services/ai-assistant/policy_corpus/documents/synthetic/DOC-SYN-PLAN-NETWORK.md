## Distinctions

- Plan/product: which benefit package is in force (for example, PPO versus HMO, Original Medicare versus Advantage, Medicaid versus a named MCO).
- Network: whether this clinic/clinician is contracted for that product.
- Eligibility: whether the person is enrolled on the date of service.

All three must be known before telling someone a visit is “in network and covered.”

## When unknown

If product is known but network is not in the current official source, say eligibility active / network unknown and escalate. Do not guess from a hallway list.
