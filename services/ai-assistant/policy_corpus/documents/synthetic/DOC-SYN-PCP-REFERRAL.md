## Rules

If the current official source says a PCP must be listed, and none is listed or the listed PCP is not this clinic, that is a referral/PCP problem, not automatically inactive coverage.

If a referral is required and absent, say eligibility may be active but referral is missing. Do not call that a denial of eligibility.

Never tell a patient that a missing referral means they are uninsured.
