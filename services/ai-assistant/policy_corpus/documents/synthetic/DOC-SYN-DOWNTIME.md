## Unavailable versus inactive

A timeout, outage, open circuit, or missing response is unavailable/unknown, never a denial.

## What to do

Use the unavailable script, document that the official source could not be reached, offer to proceed under clinic financial policy for non-emergency visits, and never delay emergency screening.

## What not to do

Do not reuse a stale active result as if it were current unless a current official last-known-good policy exists — and this training set does not invent one. Do not guess.
