## Four different numbers

Deductible remaining, copay, coinsurance, and out-of-pocket remaining are different facts. Eligibility active with deductible remaining is still active.

## Benefit limits

Visit caps, therapy caps, and exhausted benefits are not the same as terminated membership. If a limit may be exhausted, say eligibility active / benefit-limit unknown or exhausted as the official source states, and escalate if the source is silent.

## Unknown amounts

If the official response omits cost-share, say the amount is unknown. Do not quote a remembered typical copay.
