## When the spending stop fires

If the assistant cannot complete a turn because a spending limit is reached, the operational outcome is: stop, do not invent a coverage answer, and hand the visit to a human using the official payer path.

## Coverage rule still holds

A spending stop is not inactive coverage and not a denial. It is an unavailable-assistant outcome. Eligibility must be completed by a person.
