## Triggers (reverify; do not reuse the last result)

1. New visit or new episode of care.
2. Plan-year or benefit-period change.
3. Reported coverage, address, name, or subscriber change.
4. Conflicting information between card, patient statement, and last response.
5. Stale response (older than the current official payer interval, or older than this clinic’s 14-day scheduled-visit default if the payer is silent).
6. Payer or portal returned unknown/pending and a later check is now possible.
7. Medicaid/CHIP renewal month or managed-care reassignment.

## Stale response

A previously active result that is past the interval is stale, not currently active. Recheck. If the current source cannot be reached, use the unavailable script, not the old active script.
