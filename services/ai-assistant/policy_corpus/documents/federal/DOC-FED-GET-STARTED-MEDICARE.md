## Quoted eligibility categories

Medicare is health insurance for people 65 or older who meet citizenship or residency requirements. A person may be eligible earlier with a disability, End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD), or ALS. Some people get Medicare automatically; others must sign up, depending on Social Security benefits before age 65.

## Front-desk rule

Age 65 is not proof of current Medicare enrollment or of Part A/B/D/Advantage product. Verify the current product on the official source for the date of service.
