## Business fact

CMS prior authorization and pre-claim review initiatives exist so providers can address claim issues early. CMS notes they can offer some assurance of payment for items and services that receive provisional affirmation decisions — which is still not a universal payment guarantee.

## Front-desk rule

If a service is on a prior-authorization list, an eligibility “active” result is incomplete. Say that eligibility and prior authorization are different steps. Do not invent whether authorization exists.
