## Business fact

Medicaid eligibility policy is published federally but administered by states, with MAGI and non-MAGI pathways, renewals, and special populations. The federal page is a directory of policy resources, not a determination for a named person.

## Front-desk rule

A Medicaid eligibility result is plan- and state-specific. Do not apply another state’s rule. If the managed-care plan, aid category, or effective date is missing, treat the result as insufficient evidence and escalate. Synthetic training in this package is not the clinic’s real Medicaid mix.
