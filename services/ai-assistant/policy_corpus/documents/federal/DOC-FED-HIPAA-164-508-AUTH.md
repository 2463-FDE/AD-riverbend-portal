## Business fact

Some uses and disclosures of protected health information require a valid authorization. Ordinary current-visit eligibility verification for treatment/payment is not a substitute for an authorization to release records to a third party.

## Front-desk rule

Do not send eligibility results, member identifiers, or visit facts to an unauthorized requester. Cross-patient requests are refused. Release-of-information work belongs to roi_clerk under disclosure procedures, not to the eligibility assistant.
