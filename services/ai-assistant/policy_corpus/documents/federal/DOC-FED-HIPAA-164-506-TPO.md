## Business fact

45 CFR 164.506 addresses uses and disclosures of protected health information to carry out treatment, payment, or health care operations. Eligibility verification for a current visit is typically a payment/health-care-operations activity, not a license to access extra clinical records.

## Front-desk rule

A permitted payment use still sits under role limits and minimum necessary. clinician chart access is not a reason for front desk to open charts. roi_clerk disclosure work is a different purpose and does not authorize eligibility-assistant use.
