## Business fact

HHS OIG describes EMTALA as a federal law that imposes specific obligations on Medicare-participating hospitals that offer emergency services, and notes it is also known as the patient dumping statute.

## Front-desk meaning

Withholding or delaying emergency screening because eligibility is unknown, inactive, or disputed is not an acceptable operational outcome.
