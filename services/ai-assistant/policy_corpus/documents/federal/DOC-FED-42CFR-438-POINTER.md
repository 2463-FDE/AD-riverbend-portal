## Business rule

Medicaid managed-care enrollment creates plan-specific rights and network rules under 42 CFR Part 438. Front desk must not treat “Medicaid active” as “this managed-care plan and this clinic are aligned.”

If the MCO, PCP, or referral requirement is unclear, cite this regulation’s existence, cite the missing plan fact, and escalate.
