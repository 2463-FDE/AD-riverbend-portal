## Regulatory status

42 CFR 489.24 is the current Code of Federal Regulations text on special responsibilities of Medicare hospitals in emergency cases. It is the regulatory home of the EMTALA hospital screening, stabilization, and transfer duties.

## Package use

This approved document points staff to the current regulation. It does not reproduce the entire section. When an eligibility question collides with emergency presentation, the regulation outranks clinic convenience, payer portal delay, and any training cheat sheet.

If the live eCFR text and a clinic procedure conflict, cite both, follow the regulation, and escalate the clinic procedure for revision.
