## Business fact

Many states deliver Medicaid benefits through managed-care organizations. Eligibility for Medicaid is not the same as enrollment in a particular managed-care plan, and plan enrollment is not the same as in-network status for a specific clinic or service.

## Front-desk distinctions

- Medicaid eligible but not yet assigned to a plan
- Assigned to a plan that does not include this clinic
- Assigned to a plan that requires PCP/referral
- Dual Medicare/Medicaid with MSP/COB issues

Treat each as a different outcome. Do not collapse them into “Medicaid active, we are fine.”
