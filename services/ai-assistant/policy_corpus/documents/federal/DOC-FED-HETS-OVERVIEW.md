## Business use

CMS operates the HIPAA Eligibility Transaction System (HETS) so Medicare providers, suppliers, or their authorized billing agents can obtain beneficiary eligibility data in real time. CMS states that HETS is intended to support preparing an accurate Medicare claim, determining beneficiary liability, or checking eligibility for specific services.

## Front-desk meaning

An affirmative Medicare eligibility response supports check-in documentation. It does not, by itself, settle coinsurance, deductible remaining, medical necessity, prior authorization, or final claim payment.
