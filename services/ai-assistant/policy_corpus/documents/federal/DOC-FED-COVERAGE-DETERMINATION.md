## Business fact

CMS describes a coverage determination process for whether Medicare covers an item or service. That process is not the same as checking whether a beneficiary is enrolled and eligible on a date of service.

## Front-desk distinction

Eligibility: is this person covered by this product on this date?
Coverage determination / medical necessity / prior authorization: may this service be billed/paid under that product?

Do not answer a prior-authorization or coverage-determination question with an eligibility “active” flag.
