## Business fact

Medicaid.gov directs people to state-specific ways to learn whether they are eligible. Federal pages do not replace a state determination or a current managed-care enrollment file.

## Front-desk rule

Do not tell a patient they are ineligible for Medicaid based only on a clinic cheat sheet. Point them to official state/Medicaid.gov channels and verify current enrollment through the authorized payer path.
