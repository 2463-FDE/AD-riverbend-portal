## Why a pointer

42 CFR Part 435 is the federal Medicaid eligibility regulation for states, the District of Columbia, and listed territories. The retrieved text is hundreds of thousands of characters. Reproducing it here would bury the check-in rule.

## Business rule

Clinic procedures cannot override federal/state Medicaid eligibility regulation. If a cheat sheet conflicts with Part 435 or the current state source, cite both, follow the official source, and escalate the cheat sheet.
