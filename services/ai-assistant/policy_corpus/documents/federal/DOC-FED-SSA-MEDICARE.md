## Business fact

SSA explains how people plan for and enroll in Medicare. SSA enrollment pages are not a substitute for date-of-service eligibility verification with Medicare or a Medicare Advantage plan.

## Front-desk rule

“They said they signed up at Social Security” is not a verified eligibility result. Verify on the current official payer/Medicare path.
