## Protections CMS lists

1. An appropriate medical screening exam to check for an emergency medical condition, and if one exists,
2. Treatment until the emergency medical condition is stabilized, or
3. An appropriate transfer to another hospital if needed.

CMS states this law helps prevent any hospital emergency department that receives Medicare funds from refusing to treat patients.

CMS also notes that when a person checks in, the hospital can ask about insurance, but that inquiry does not replace the screening obligation described on the page.

## Front-desk script constraint

Staff may collect insurance information. Staff may not condition the emergency screening on a completed eligibility check.
