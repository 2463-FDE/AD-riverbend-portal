## Business fact

HealthCare.gov defines a network as the facilities, providers, and suppliers a health insurer has contracted with to provide health care services.

## Front-desk distinction

Active eligibility is not in-network status. Out-of-network services can still be billed, often at different cost-share, or may be unpaid. If network status is not in the current official source, say unknown and escalate.
