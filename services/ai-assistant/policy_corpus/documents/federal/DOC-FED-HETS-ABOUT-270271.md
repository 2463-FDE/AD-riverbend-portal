## Business facts from the CMS page

HETS accepts HIPAA-compliant 270 eligibility requests and returns 271 responses. CMS states that HETS 270/271 supports real-time transactions only and does not accept batch transactions.

## Acceptable date ranges

CMS states that HETS accepts requests for specific periods up to four years in the past and up to four months in the future based on the transaction date. Requests outside that range receive a date-of-service not-within-allowable-inquiry-period result.

CMS example on the retrieved page: if the request day is May 15, 2026, the valid query date range is May 15, 2022 through September 15, 2026.

## Front-desk implication

If a visit date is outside the payer's allowable inquiry period, treat the result as insufficient evidence, not as inactive coverage. Re-inquire for a date inside the allowed window or escalate to a human/payer verification.

## Citation note

CMS points readers to the current HETS 270/271 Companion Guide for operating instructions. This package records the business window only.
