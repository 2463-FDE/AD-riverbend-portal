## Business fact

Medigap is supplemental insurance that works with Original Medicare. It is not Medicare Advantage. A person generally cannot have Medigap and Medicare Advantage in the usual overlapping way described on Medicare.gov.

## Front-desk rule

If both a Medicare Advantage card and a Medigap card appear, treat the combination as a conflict/insufficient evidence until a human confirms which product is actually in force.
