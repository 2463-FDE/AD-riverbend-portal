## Definitions from the retrieved page

- Deductible: the amount you pay for covered health care services before your insurance plan starts to pay.
- Copayment: a fixed amount you pay for a plan-covered service.
- Coinsurance: a percentage of the cost that you pay for each plan-covered service.

The page notes that many plans pay for certain services before the deductible, that Marketplace plans pay the full cost of certain preventive benefits before the deductible, and that some plans have separate deductibles (for example, drugs) and family versus individual deductibles.
