## Business fact

Medicare.gov describes Medicare Advantage and other Medicare health plans as distinct from Original Medicare. Network, prior authorization, and referral behavior often differ.

## Front-desk rule

Never answer an MA coverage question from Original Medicare benefit pages alone. Cite the current plan source or escalate.
