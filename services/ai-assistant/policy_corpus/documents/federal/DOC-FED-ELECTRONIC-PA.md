## Business fact

CMS, health plans, and certified EHR vendors work to streamline electronic prior authorization for medical items and services. CMS describes prior authorization as a burden-heavy, separate request process.

## Front-desk rule

Never equate a successful eligibility check with a completed prior authorization. If a clerk asks both questions, answer them separately or escalate the authorization question.
