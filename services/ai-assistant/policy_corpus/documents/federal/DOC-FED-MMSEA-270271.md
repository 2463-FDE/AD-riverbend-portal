## Business fact

CMS publishes a 270/271 Health Care Eligibility Benefit Inquiry and Response companion guide for mandatory Section 111 group health plan reporting. The 270 transaction set is used to transmit health care eligibility benefit inquiries; it can be used to inquire about Medicare eligibility of an individual.

## Front-desk limit

This is federal context that eligibility inquiries are a standard business transaction. It is not authority to reproduce X12 specifications, and it is not a clinic payer mix.
