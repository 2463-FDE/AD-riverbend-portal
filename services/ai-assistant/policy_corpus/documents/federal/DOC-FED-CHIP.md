## Business fact

CHIP is a distinct federal-state program for children. Some states administer it alongside Medicaid; product names, networks, and verification paths can differ.

## Front-desk rule

Do not label CHIP as Medicaid or Medicare. If the card or response names CHIP or a state child health plan, verify that product. If the product is unclear, escalate rather than guessing.
