## Binding business rule (CMS disclosure)

The CMS HETS 270/271 Companion Guide disclosure states that disclosure of Medicare beneficiary eligibility data is restricted under the Privacy Act of 1974 and HIPAA, and that the provider Medicare beneficiary eligibility transaction is to be used for conducting Medicare business only.

CMS states, in the same disclosure: the 271 response returned by the HETS 270/271 application should not be interpreted as a guarantee of payment. Payment of benefits remains subject to all health benefit plan terms, limits, conditions, exclusions, and the member’s eligibility at the time services are rendered.

## Front-desk language

When Medicare eligibility is active, staff may say that eligibility was confirmed for the stated date of service and cite this disclosure. Staff must never say that Medicare or any other payer has guaranteed payment.

The companion guide is a large operating specification. This approved document records only the payment-guarantee and permitted-use disclosure. Transaction maps, connectivity, and companion-guide implementation detail are intentionally omitted.
