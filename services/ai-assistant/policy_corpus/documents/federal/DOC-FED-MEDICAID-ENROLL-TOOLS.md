## Business fact

Federal Medicaid resources distinguish eligibility, enrollment, and renewal. Coverage can end or change at renewal even if last month’s verification was active.

## Reverification trigger

A new eligibility period, renewal month, or plan-year change is a reverification trigger. Do not reuse a stale Medicaid/CHIP result across a renewal boundary.
