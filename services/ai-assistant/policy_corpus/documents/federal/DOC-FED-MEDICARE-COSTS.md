## Business fact

Medicare.gov explains premiums, deductibles, copayments, and coinsurance as different kinds of patient cost. Those amounts change by year and by product (Original Medicare versus Medicare Advantage versus Part D).

## Front-desk rule

Do not quote last year’s numbers as this year’s. Do not treat “deductible not met” as inactive coverage. If remaining deductible is not in the current official response, say the cost-share is unknown and escalate rather than inventing a dollar figure.
