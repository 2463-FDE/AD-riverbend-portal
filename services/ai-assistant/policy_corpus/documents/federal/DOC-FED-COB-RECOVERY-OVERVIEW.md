## Business fact

CMS states that the Medicare Secondary Payer (MSP) program exists to ensure Medicare is aware of situations where it should not be the primary, or first, payer of claims. If a beneficiary has Medicare and other health insurance, COB rules decide which entity pays first.

## Front-desk implication

Ask whether other coverage exists (employer, retiree, accident, workers’ compensation, liability, Medicaid). An eligibility response that shows Medicare active does not prove Medicare is primary. Missing other-coverage information is insufficient evidence, not proof that Medicare pays first.
