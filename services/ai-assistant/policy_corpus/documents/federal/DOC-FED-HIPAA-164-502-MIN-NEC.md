## Quoted standard

When using or disclosing protected health information or when requesting protected health information from another covered entity or business associate, a covered entity or business associate must make reasonable efforts to limit protected health information to the minimum necessary to accomplish the intended purpose of the use, disclosure, or request.

## Stated exceptions (do not over-read)

The minimum necessary requirement does not apply to disclosures to or requests by a health care provider for treatment; uses or disclosures to the individual; uses or disclosures under an authorization; disclosures to the Secretary; uses or disclosures required by law as described in § 164.512(a); and other listed exceptions in the current regulation. Eligibility check-in for ordinary scheduled visits is not an invitation to browse charts.

## Front-desk eligibility implication

Ask only for identifiers and coverage facts needed to verify eligibility for the current visit. Do not retrieve clinical notes, other patients’ records, or extra family details “in case they help.” The Riverbend front_desk role has no chart access; that shipped access rule implements this minimum-necessary idea for registration work.
