## Business fact

Medicare Advantage (Part C) is a Medicare product delivered by private plans. CMS publishes enrollment and plan resources separately from Original Medicare eligibility.

## Front-desk distinctions

- Original Medicare A/B active is not proof of Medicare Advantage enrollment.
- Medicare Advantage enrollment is not proof this clinic is in that plan’s network.
- Plan directories and referral rules are plan-specific and change.

If the product is MA, verify that plan, not generic Medicare.
