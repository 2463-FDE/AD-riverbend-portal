## Business fact

Medicare.gov’s Coordination of Benefits publication explains that if a person has Medicare and other health or drug coverage, each type of coverage is called a payer. When there is more than one potential payer, coordination rules decide who pays first.

## Front-desk language

Staff may explain that two coverages do not automatically mean double payment, and that one payer is usually primary. Staff must not invent which payer is primary. If the patient disputes the order, record the dispute and escalate.
