## What CMS says the BCRC does

The Benefits Coordination & Recovery Center (BCRC) consolidates collection, management, and reporting of other insurance coverage for Medicare beneficiaries. CMS states the COB program’s purpose is to identify health benefits available to a Medicare beneficiary and coordinate payment to prevent mistaken Medicare payment. The BCRC does not process claims or claim-specific inquiries.

## What local Medicare claims offices do

Medicare Administrative Contractors process claims submitted for primary or secondary payment and resolve mistaken Medicare payments. Billing-how-to questions stay with the local claims office.

## Provider data duty

CMS relies on providers and suppliers to ask Medicare patients about other primary health coverage and to report that information when filing claims. Beneficiary, spouse, or family employment changes, accidents, illness or injury, federal program coverage changes, or other insurance information should be reported through the BCRC channels CMS names.

## Privacy at BCRC contact

CMS states that before releasing protected information about a beneficiary, BCRC needs identifying information that matches its records; otherwise it will answer only general COB or MSP questions.

## Front-desk takeaway

Eligibility staff collect and flag other-coverage clues. They do not declare MSP order when facts are incomplete, and they do not treat an eligibility “active” flag as a paid claim.
