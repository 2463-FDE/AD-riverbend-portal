## Business definition

CMS states that coordination of benefits (COB) allows plans that provide health and/or prescription coverage for a person with Medicare to determine their respective payment responsibilities: which plan has primary payment responsibility and the extent to which other plans contribute when a person is covered by more than one plan.

## Front-desk rule

When more than one coverage appears, record both, do not silently pick a favorite, and do not tell the patient that “Medicare always pays first.” Primary versus secondary order depends on the specific coverage types (employer group, retiree, liability, workers’ compensation, Medicaid, and others) under Medicare Secondary Payer rules.

If the order is unclear, cite this CMS COB overview, state the conflict or missing fact, and route to human/payer verification. Do not invent a primary payer.
