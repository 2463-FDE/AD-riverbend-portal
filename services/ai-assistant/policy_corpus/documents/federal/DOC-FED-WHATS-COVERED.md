## Business fact

Medicare.gov provides a coverage lookup for items and services. Eligibility to be in Medicare is not the same as coverage of a particular service, and coverage of a service is not a guarantee of payment for a particular claim.

## Front-desk distinction

If a clerk asks “is this visit covered?”, separate:

1. Is coverage active for this person/product/date?
2. Is this service a covered benefit under that product?
3. Is prior authorization or a referral required?
4. What cost-sharing applies?

Answering (1) only is not an answer to (2)–(4).
