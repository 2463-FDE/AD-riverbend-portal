  **The retrieval record (SPEC-63, ⚠ REQ-19).** `policy_index.lookup` and `fetch_by_id` return a `LookupRecord` — a **frozen dataclass of fourteen closed fields**: per axis (`topic`, `payer`, `product`, `state`) the resolved value, typed `str | None`, and a provenance label drawn from the three-value set `clerk_selection` · `model_topic` · `application_default`, plus the integers `pre_filter_rows`, `post_filter_rows`, `returned_rows`, `cap` and the booleans `truncated`, `empty`.
