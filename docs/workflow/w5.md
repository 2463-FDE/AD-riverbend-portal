## Requirements

Status: DRAFT
Source: engagement owner ask, 2026-08-17

> Our scheduling keeps double-booking the same slot and it's getting embarrassing — two
> patients show up for one appointment. The team's pretty sure it's a glitch in the calendar
> widget, so could you just fix the calendar so it stops doing that? I pulled some example
> rows and the booking code for you. It seems to happen more when the system's busy.
> — Dr. Maya Okonkwo, COO — Riverbend Community Health
>
> Handed over: appointments.csv (two rows, both slot_id=88231, both status=confirmed,
> created_at 09:31:04.120 and 09:31:04.519, different patient IDs) · billing ticket RIV-175
> ("probably a calendar glitch") · scheduling-service/book.py · a server log showing a 2.11s
> POST /appointments for slot 88231 and a second POST for the same slot ~400ms later, both
> returning 201 · a frontend network tab showing no idempotency key / request-id header ·
> the appointments DDL (slot_id INTEGER, no UNIQUE, no composite constraint).

**Problem scoping (the reframe the deliverable asks for).** The two confirmed rows are two
successful server-side inserts, not a rendering artifact: `book.py:81` is a
read-check-then-insert with no transaction and no UNIQUE on `slot_id`, so two
near-simultaneous requests (or a client retry of a slow POST — no idempotency key exists to
dedupe one) both pass `slot_taken()` and both insert — "worse when busy" is the race
signature. The service compounds it: a taken slot still answers HTTP 201 (body
`status="slot_taken"`, `services/scheduling-service/app.py:125`). And `docs/debt-log.md` D5b
records a second, race-free path: nothing anywhere writes `Slot.status`, so a booked slot
stays in `GET /slots` forever and two users minutes apart can both book it — a UNIQUE
constraint alone does not close RIV-175. No calendar-widget change can fix any of this.

|----|-------------|-------|
| w5-REQ-1 | At most one confirmed appointment exists per slot; the invariant holds under concurrent booking requests and under load, enforced server-side regardless of portal behavior | ⚠ human-gate (landmines §1 booking-race zone; enforcement implies a schema/migration change). D5b |
