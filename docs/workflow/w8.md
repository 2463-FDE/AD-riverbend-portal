> **WHAT THEY HANDED OVER** — Your AI summary currently sends the full encounter record {name, dob, mrn, notes} to the cloud LLM vendor — nothing is stripped before it leaves the building. · Nobody has checked whether the model provider you chose is covered by a Business Associate Agreement, or what its data-retention terms actually say — that question has never been asked in writing. · A sample row from that 'de-identified' analytics export: {zip:[ZIP-REDACTED-1], dob:[DOB-REDACTED-1], mrn:[MRN-REDACTED-1], device_id:'IPAD-07', visit_date:'2026-06-14'}. · HHS Safe-Harbor reference: the list of 18 identifiers that must be removed for de-identification. · No data-flow diagram of what leaves the PHI boundary, or under what agreement.
>
> **QUESTIONS TO DIG INTO** — Trace exactly which fields leave Riverbend when the AI summary runs — and is there a signed BAA covering that vendor? · Check the 'de-identified' export against the 18 Safe-Harbor identifiers — how many are still present? · Could you re-identify someone from {ZIP + DOB + visit date}? (it's famously few fields.) · What has to happen to data BEFORE it crosses to a vendor for this feature to be lawful?
>
> **CURRENT PROBLEMS (STATED / KNOWN)** — Board wants automatic visit-summaries for every patient. · Client believes the analytics export is already 'de-identified.'
>
> **THIS WEEK'S DELIVERABLE** — A responsible-AI design package: a Safe-Harbor de-identification scrub (18 identifiers, unit-tested redactor) used before any LLM/analytics path + a data-flow + BAA/vendor-governance memo (what leaves the PHI boundary, under what agreement) + a recommendation gate.
>
> **⚠ QUOTA / SCOPE RISK** — Multi-field LLM scrub — test the redactor offline with unit tests, not by spamming the LLM.

*(Redaction note: the sample-row `zip`, `dob`, `mrn` values are replaced per the stage-1 redact-then-quote rule — DOB and MRN are `docs/phi-logging-policy.md` rule-2 identifiers, ZIP is address-class. `device_id`/`visit_date` are not rule-2 and are kept for the re-identification analysis the ask requests.)*
